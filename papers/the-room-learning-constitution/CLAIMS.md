## Non-claims

This package does not claim that all opinions are equally good, all evidence deserves equal weight, every disagreement must remain open indefinitely, all exclusions are illegitimate, correctability guarantees truth, the five constitutional clauses are uniquely minimal, or the proposed Article X is already existing international law.
