## Explicit non-claims

This paper does **not** claim novelty for:

- viability kernels, admissible controls, or controlled invariance;
- organizational closure, autopoiesis, adaptivity, or biological normativity;
- biological regulation as second-order control;
- common-cause reliability limits;
- Shapley or Harsanyi attribution;
- adaptive fungal transport or sublinear/superlinear reinforcement thresholds;
- the verifier's dilemma or monitoring/reputation mechanisms in cooperation;
- the proposition that every persistent network instantiates the seven-part decomposition.

The intended contribution is the diagnostic composition of these ideas around distributed maintenance, together with the function–renewal proxy-gap hypothesis and a concrete empirical testing programme.
