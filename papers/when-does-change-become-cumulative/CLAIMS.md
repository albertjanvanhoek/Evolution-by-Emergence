## Non-claims

The paper does not claim that:

- evolution generally preserves every capability;
- all target families are scientifically equivalent;
- larger accessible sets are intrinsically better;
- complexity is monotone;
- recombination, transfer, symbiosis, or cross-scale incorporation always expand accessibility;
- pointwise score or cost dominance is necessary for a click;
- cost is the definition of organizational accessibility;
- the abstract order relation alone proves that a particular empirical system exhibits a historically caused ratchet;
- infinitely many fixed-resolution clicks can occur physically in finite time.
