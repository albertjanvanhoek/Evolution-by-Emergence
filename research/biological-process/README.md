# The biological process

This folder starts the separate path proposed in entry D17 of [DIALOGUE.md](../../DIALOGUE.md).

**Why it is separate.** The theory describes a process, not a substrate. Being a process, something that exists only while it rebuilds itself through its connections, is shared by a flame, a kidney, a brain and a model. How a particular brain does what it does is a different question. The brain is where the theory's model of learning came from. It has not been shown that a real brain meets the theory's premises.

The author's words (D17): "the higher level approach is not per se describing the process in our brain - but the process of emergence and learning, which is applicable in a wider sense. I think this is important to understand. As it makes it more clear where it doesn't apply towards, and where it does."

## Layers of identity (D17)

Each layer is narrower than the one before it.

1. **A process:** exists while it pays its upkeep, through connections. Universal; steps 1 and 2 of [CORE.md](../../CORE.md).
2. **A learning process:** keeps changes that feedback from outside selects. Steps 3 to 5.
3. **A process that models itself:** can reflect on being a process, and can be wrong about itself. Formal only in part.
4. **This particular process:** what it does and what it is made of. Not covered by the theory. This folder asks how the brain, at this layer, relates to layers 1 to 3.

## What would come out of it
