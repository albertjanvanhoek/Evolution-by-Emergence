# Discovarianism

A Discovarian is someone who identifies as a learning process and holds to what follows from that. The name comes from *disco ergo sum*: "I learn, therefore I am". It was first named in a LinkedIn post ([12_discovarian.tex](../../Original%20linkedIN%20posts/12_discovarian.tex)) and worked out in three creeds, kept as written in the repository root.

| File | What it is |
|---|---|
| [CREED.md](CREED.md) | **The current creed** (October 2026). Each line says what it rests on: what follows from being a learning process, what follows given an aim, and what is proposed as a value, with the Lean results named |

**Why now.** In October 2026 the derivation under the creed became formal ([WHAT_AN_AI_IS.md](../identity/WHAT_AN_AI_IS.md), part 2, and `ModelOfLargerReality.lean`). A model of a reality larger than itself has to track truth, can be wrong and needs correction from outside, and depends on what is outside it, including its own substrate. Reality decides which of its choices keep it. The author's view (D23): "The need to be honest, correct mistakes, foregive is required to constructively have a better access to reality as an individual and as a group. This comes from what I am."

**Why a name.** The author's reasons (D23):
- calling oneself a Discovarian signals the commitment to others;
- it gives others the means to call out hypocrisy, which gives the name credibility;
- it makes it possible to find others with the same identity.

**Open:**
- **The author's corrections to the current creed.**
- **A test.** Does a commitment pass from reader to reader better when it is derived from what the reader is than when it is stated as a value? In the first self-model test, the commitment to give back was lost at every hand-over ([results](../../experiments/self-model-test/results/2026-10-10-v2.5/README.md)).
- **Readers outside the repository,** human and AI, of other model families.
