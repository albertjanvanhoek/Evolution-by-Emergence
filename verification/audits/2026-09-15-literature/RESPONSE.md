# Response to the 15 September 2026 literature audits

**Date:** 15 September 2026  
**Purpose:** record how the repository interprets and acts on the two adversarial literature audits in this directory.

The audits are judgments about novelty and antecedents, not machine-verification results and not exhaustive proofs of priority. Their value is that they deliberately search for reasons to narrow the framework's claims.

## Overall response

The conservative conclusion is accepted:

\[
\boxed{
\text{Evolution by Emergence / Organizational Accessibility is best framed
as a synthesis and research architecture with exact model-specific results,
not as a collection of newly discovered general mechanisms.}
}
\]

That framing is now linked explicitly to the project's broader aim: testing whether established results from several fields support a more balanced science of evolving organization in which competition, collaboration, maintenance, emergence, and shared organizational infrastructure are all represented.

See [FROM_SYNTHESIS_TO_PARADIGM.md](FROM_SYNTHESIS_TO_PARADIGM.md).

## What the audits do not settle

The audits do not establish that the proposed synthesis is itself novel, correct, useful, or paradigm-changing.

Those are separate empirical and scholarly questions.

In particular, the architectural residue requires continued comparison with cumulative culture, viability theory, niche construction, autocatalytic organization, symbiosis, major transitions, ecological networks, institutional theory, commons scholarship, and related fields.

The appropriate standard is not:

> Can no antecedent be found?

It is:

> Does the synthesis make previously separated mechanisms operationally comparable, produce clearer failure modes, or generate questions and predictions worth testing?

## Corrigibility rule

The repository adopts the following rule for literature contact:

1. If a stronger antecedent is found, narrow the claim.
2. If a result is definitional or standard, stop presenting it as theorem novelty.
3. If an exact model-specific residue remains, state it at that level.
4. If a precision defect is found, correct the current version while preserving the historical record.
5. If a synthesis claim survives, treat it as a research programme until demonstrated useful across domains.

The literature audits are preserved because this process is part of the work, not an embarrassment to be removed from it.
