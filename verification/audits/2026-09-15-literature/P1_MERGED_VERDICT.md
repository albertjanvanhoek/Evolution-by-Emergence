Plus **Margolus & Levitin 1998** (doi:10.1016/S0167-2789(98)00054-2), which already concludes that finite energy and finite time permit only finitely many distinguishable states.

## The harder antecedents, by result

**1 — R_org.** Not merely "NGM-shaped." **Guo, Li & Shuai's multigroup SIR R₀ = (Λ/μ)·ρ(B)/d is the identical formula** under an exact variable mapping (J↔Λ, ℓ↔μ, d↔removal, B↔β), with the same uniqueness theorem. And **Johnston & Avram 2026** (doi:10.1007/s00285-026-02351-z) already published NGM-for-chemical-reaction-networks, routing autocatalytic terms into F exactly as here. Worst of all: **Sraffa's "self-replacing state" (1960) and the Hawkins–Simon condition ρ(A) < 1 (1949)** make "nobody has a threshold for collective self-replacement" false since 1949. What survives: COT and RAF genuinely have no threshold quantity, so the bridge is real — but it is a bridge, not a derivation.

**2 — fold / J_fold < J_inv.** The closer antecedent is not backward bifurcation but **Gray & Scott 1983** (doi:10.1016/0009-2509(83)80132-8): cubic autocatalysis in a CSTR, with exact parametric multiplicity conditions. Same mathematical object, forty-three years earlier. This is the citation a reviewer reaches for first.

**3 — mean fitness pinned.** Beyond chemostat μ = D, this is **Fisher's own argument** — the gain from selection is offset by "deterioration of the environment," so mean fitness "cannot greatly exceed zero" (via Frank & Slatkin 1992). It is also the founding axiom of adaptive dynamics, and the **pessimization principle** (Mylius & Diekmann 1995) says evolution minimises R\* — which is exactly what happens here. Separately: **Cov(w,r) = 0 is a tautology, not a theorem**, and should be dropped.

**4 — five coordinates.** **Mylius & Diekmann 1995** (doi:10.2307/3545651) already prove r, R₀ and invasion fitness are non-equivalent *in a dynamical model*; **Dolson et al. 2019 MODES** (doi:10.1162/artl_a_00280) demonstrate four candidate organizational measures dissociating in running systems. **Fath, Patten & Choi 2001** reached the *opposite* verdict — that ecological goal functions are mutually complementary — which is genuinely useful: the stack dissents from a standing claim. And (v), functional adequacy requires a declared function and threshold, is **Cummins 1975** plus **Hazen et al. 2007**, where functional information is *defined* only after declaring both.

**5 — κ_crit = M⁰.** Two textbook halves. The 1/(1+κ) law is **Qian et al. 2017** and **Gyorgy et al. 2015** isocost lines, verbatim. And "tolerable load equals pre-existing margin" is the **structural-engineering margin of safety, MS = FS − 1**, imported to biology by **Diamond 2002**. It is a restatement of a definition, not a theorem.

**6 — master alignment inequality.** It is `∂V/∂k ≥ 0` evaluated at the constraint — the definition of a non-binding constraint. My auditor derived it directly: with z\* = η/(γ+βk), dz\*/dk|_{k_func} = −βm²/η, giving βm²Φ′(m) ≥ ηC′(k_func) mechanically.

**7 — the no-go.** Three defects beyond the priority problem. The **d_op ≤ d_TV step is empty** — d_TV is already a supremum over measurements, so this is the data-processing inequality plus relabelling. There is a **factor-of-4 discrepancy** with the cited Shiraishi–Funo–Saito bound (their Eq. 8 is L² ≤ 2Σ⟨A⟩τ, likely an L₁-vs-TV convention, but as written the citation does not match). And the load-bearing assumption is uniformity of c_n — drop it and you get **Feller's 1940 explosion criterion**, meaning the result does not exclude infinite depth, only infinite depth *at fixed resolution*, which is exactly the escape every supertask construction already uses.

**8 — retention vs cost domination.** **A precision defect a referee will catch immediately.** "(D) implies (R) for every budget; the converse fails" is false as written: if (R) holds for *every* B, take B = c_t(x) and (D) follows. (D) and (R)-for-all-B are **equivalent**. The non-equivalence lives only at a fixed single budget. Restate as "(D) ⟺ (R) at every budget; at a fixed budget (R) is strictly weaker."

**9 — two-click witness.** **Lane & Martin 2010** (doi:10.1038/nature09486) is the same claim, quantitatively, in *Nature*: mitochondrial acquisition raised the energy budget per gene by four to six orders of magnitude, making genomic complexity affordable when it was not before. And **Kiyotaki & Moore 1997 "Credit Cycles"** (doi:10.1086/262072) is the exact formalization of "an earlier acquisition financing a later one out of a shared budget" — assets acquired at t serve as collateral relaxing the constraint at t+1, opening a newly-feasible interval structurally identical to (M₀, M₁].

**10 — winding/spending.** Definitional. And E[log r] < 0 is **Lewontin & Cohen 1969** (doi:10.1073/pnas.62.4.1056) — a population with E[λ] > 1 goes extinct a.s. if E[log λ] < 0 — plus Kelly 1956. Fifty-seven years old.

**11 — affinity volcano.** The identity is decisive: **4a/(1+a)² ≡ 4θ(1−θ)** with θ = a/(1+a) the Langmuir occupancy. It is the textbook two-step adsorb-then-release rate law, and peak-at-a=1 is the symmetric special case of a published family (the general optimum is θ\* = α, Murzin 2020). The a → 1/a symmetry is **impedance mismatch loss**, 4Z₁Z₂/(Z₁+Z₂)². And there is a **terminology collision**: *Commun. Phys.* 7 (2024), doi:10.1038/s42005-024-01704-8, already published an optimal *affinity* inside an autocatalytic network — different quantity, indistinguishable headline. Disambiguate in the abstract or this reads as rediscovery even though it isn't.

**12 — filtering.** Elementary truncation. The composition-versus-rate distinction is the structure of **Ohta's nearly-neutral theory** and the drift barrier, and appears in biology as **Giovannoni et al. 2014** streamlining (doi:10.1038/ismej.2014.60) and in economics as **Acemoglu 2010** on directed technical change.

## The architecture claim (P3, answered early)

Both audits found the same falsifier, independently: **Mesoudi 2011, "Variable Cultural Acquisition Costs Constrain Cumulative Cultural Evolution"** (doi:10.1371/journal.pone.0018239) has all three legs — a declared dependent trait repertoire that must be retained, an explicit effort budget λ, and an acquisition cost that is *a function of the previous generation's accumulated skill*. So "cumulative culture has ratchets but no resource accounting" is false.

This audit found two further instantiations in other registers: **Kirkpatrick et al. 2017 EWC** (doi:10.1073/pnas.1611835114) — declared task repertoire, fixed capacity, penalty derived from the network's own prior learning; and **Repenning & Sterman 2002 capability traps** (doi:10.2307/3094806).

One correction to the framework's own map: **Montévil & Mossio is not the closest candidate.** Read against the three criteria it is two-of-three and entirely non-quantitative — functions are emergent from closure rather than declared, and the authors explicitly reject a thermodynamic description.

**Surviving residue:** the conjunction with a *material production budget*, a *hard* retention constraint rather than a soft penalty, and a Perron–Frobenius viability margin. That is a register nobody occupies. It is a sharpening, not the "no one has all three" claim.

## What this means

The stack is a **synthesis with a handful of exact model-specific results**, and it should be written as one. That is not nothing — it is what the failure criterion was written to detect, and detecting it before submission is the entire point of running P1 first.

Three results retain defensible residue: the closed-form fold/invasion separation (2), the constructive counterexample where several endogenous coordinates improve *while the network is gutted* (4), and the exact two-click budget witness (9). Each should be framed as an exact instance of a named phenomenon, with the antecedent cited in the first paragraph.

Three defects should be fixed regardless of framing: the quantifier error in Result 8, the factor-of-4 in the SFS citation, and the empty d_op ≤ d_TV step.

And one claim should be retired outright: `Cov(w,r) = 0` is a definition, not a finding.
