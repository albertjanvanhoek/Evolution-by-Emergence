# Independent prior-art preflight for v17

**Target:** PR #60, frozen candidate head `cb43c79631d78ec51d0aa077750c01c1120eda92`  
**Date:** 19 September 2026  
**Purpose:** try to find an equal-or-stronger antecedent to the **full v17 factorization**, not merely a neighboring theory that contains one or two of its mechanisms.

## Method

This preflight deliberately started from the literature rather than from EbE terminology.

Searches were run across:

- open-ended evolution / artificial life;
- autocatalytic-set and RAF theory;
- Self-Other Reorganization;
- cumulative cultural evolution;
- technological recombination and bounded search;
- co-evolving technological systems and search spaces;
- changing phase spaces / adjacent possible;
- constructor and component-reuse formalisms;
- formal systems-construction literature.

The hostile comparison target was the following conjunction:

```text
A. finite-parent generation or construction
B. configuration-level realization of a capacity/function
C. relative compositional emergence
D. resource / feasibility constraint
E. external validation / selection kept distinct
F. actual retention
G. retained product reused as later parent material
H. essential-parent counterfactual for changed generability
I. finite admission / bounded local search
J. full recursive successor condition / deterministic successor count
K. cumulative operational and/or historical novelty
L. fixed-finite saturation boundary
M. finite moving local envelope / changing effective search horizon
N. explicit formal separation/countermodels between these interfaces
O. machine-checked theorem surface
```

A paper or theory was treated as an **equal-or-stronger duplicate** only if it
covered essentially the whole conjunction in one coherent formal architecture.

Broad thematic similarity was not enough.

# Strongest antecedent 1 — generative RAF theory

## References

Steel M, Hordijk W.  
*Tractable models of self-sustaining autocatalytic networks.*  
arXiv:1801.03953 (2018).  
https://arxiv.org/abs/1801.03953

Huson DH, Xavier JC, Steel M.  
*Self-generating autocatalytic networks: structural results, algorithms and
their relevance to early biochemistry.*  
J R Soc Interface. 2024;21:20230732.  
doi:10.1098/rsif.2023.0732

Gabora L, Steel M.  
*An evolutionary process without variation and selection.*  
J R Soc Interface. 2021;18:20210334.  
doi:10.1098/rsif.2021.0334

Gabora L, Steel M.  
*Cumulative, Adaptive, Open-ended Change through Self-Other Reorganization:
Reply to comment on 'An evolutionary process without variation and selection'.*  
arXiv:2410.05294 (2024).

## Why this is the strongest challenge

Generative RAFs explicitly formalize networks that can be built up from simpler
RAFs.

RAF theory also formalizes:

- food-generated construction;
- autocatalytic closure;
- self-sustaining organization;
- production of components that participate in later reactions;
- hierarchical build-up;
- ordering constraints among reactions;
- in cognitive/cultural applications, newly generated representations changing
  what later cognitive structures can form.

Self-Other Reorganization goes further conceptually by using RAFs as a common
framework for cumulative, adaptive and potentially open-ended change in early
life and culture.

So the following broad claim is **not an EbE novelty**:

```text
existing organized network
    -> generates new elements / structures
    -> those products alter future configuration possibilities
    -> cumulative organization can result.
```

## Where it does not appear to duplicate v17

In the material inspected, RAF/SOR does not provide the same explicit generic
factorization of all of:

```text
relative compositional capacity emergence
!= resource feasibility
!= external validation
!= actual retention
!= operational promotion
!= essential-parent counterfactual
!= finite admission
!= deterministic full-successor count
!= active/history split
!= fixed-finite accumulated-repertoire no-go
!= finite moving local envelope.
```

RAF theory is substantially **stronger and more specific** than EbE in its
native chemical/autocatalytic domain.

EbE should therefore defer to RAF theory whenever RAF structure is the actual
mechanism.

The possible EbE residue is the cross-domain separation of interfaces, not a
new theory of autocatalysis.

## Important caution

SOR itself is contested.

Zachar et al. (2024) argue that the original SOR adaptive-evolution claim relies
on restrictive assumptions and becomes trivial or Darwinian once degradation,
growth and maladaptive effects are introduced.

That criticism does not remove RAF theory as prior art, but it means EbE should
not use SOR as uncontested evidence that open-ended adaptive evolution follows
from communal autocatalytic reorganization.

**Preflight classification:** strongest mechanistic antecedent; not a full
duplicate found.

# Strongest antecedent 2 — Soros / Stanley open-ended-evolution conditions

## References

Soros LB, Stanley KO.  
*Identifying Necessary Conditions for Open-Ended Evolution through the
Artificial Life World of Chromaria.*  
ALIFE 14 (2014), pp. 793–800.  
doi:10.7551/978-0-262-32621-6-ch128

Soros LB.  
*Necessary Conditions for Open-Ended Evolution.*  
PhD dissertation, University of Central Florida, 2018.

Dolson EL et al. / OEE literature summarized in:  
*The MODES Toolbox: Measurements of Open-Ended Dynamics in Evolving Systems.*  
Artificial Life. 2019;25(1):50–73.  
doi:10.1162/artl_a_00280

## Why this is a serious antecedent

The Chromaria programme explicitly hypothesizes that open-ended evolution
requires, among other things:

```text
a nontrivial minimal criterion before reproduction
AND
newly evolved individuals create new ways/opportunities to satisfy that criterion
AND
interaction decisions are endogenous
AND
phenotypic potential is unbounded in principle.
```

The second condition is especially close to EbE's recursive-accessibility
intuition:

> successful new organization changes the opportunities available to future
> organization.

Chromaria also uses controlled ablations to test whether removal of such
conditions produces stagnation.

Thus EbE cannot claim priority for the idea:

```text
new successful individuals must create new future opportunities
for open-ended evolution.
```

## Residual difference

Chromaria/OEE work asks for conditions or hallmarks of open-ended evolutionary
systems.

The v17 object instead decomposes one local event chain in more detail:

```text
generation
-> emergence
-> feasibility
-> validation
-> retention
-> reuse
-> changed generability
-> finite admission
-> actual recursive successor.
```

The EbE finite-saturation and moving-envelope results also operate on cumulative
retained repertoire/envelope sets rather than on the full complexity/diversity
hallmark programme of OEE.

**Preflight classification:** very strong conceptual/experimental antecedent;
not a full factorized duplicate found.

# Strongest antecedent 3 — co-evolving technological systems and search spaces

## Reference

Winters J, Charbonneau M.  
*Modelling the emergence of open-ended technological evolution.*  
arXiv:2508.04828 (2025).  
https://arxiv.org/abs/2508.04828

## Why it matters

This model makes a point unusually close to the v17 moving-accessibility story.

It explicitly treats:

- a technological system as a repertoire of interdependent skills,
  techniques and artifacts;
- a search space as needs/problems/goals;
- both technological systems and search spaces as evolving;
- effectiveness/selection and stochastic novelty as jointly relevant;
- open-ended technological growth as depending on sustained co-evolution
  between the repertoire and the search space.

This is a strong antecedent for:

```text
retained technology changes the future search situation,
and open-ended technological evolution cannot be understood as movement
through one static search landscape.
```

## Residual difference

The Winters–Charbonneau model is a macro-dynamical model.

The v17 formal core is an event-factorization/audit architecture.

The inspected technological model does not appear to separate, in one theorem
surface:

- compositional emergence from validation;
- capacity-level generation from configuration-level construction;
- essential parent contribution from mere participation;
- all generable possibilities from finite admission;
- deterministic actual recursive successor count;
- operational repertoire from historical trace.

**Preflight classification:** strongest current technological antecedent; not a
full duplicate found.

# Strongest antecedent 4 — cumulative culture / cognitive RAFs

## References

Enquist M, Ghirlanda S, Eriksson K.  
*Modelling the evolution and diversity of cumulative culture.*  
Phil Trans R Soc B. 2011;366:412–423.  
doi:10.1098/rstb.2010.0132

Gabora L et al.  
*Modeling a Cognitive Transition at the Origin of Cultural Evolution Using
Autocatalytic Networks.*  
Cognitive Science. 2020.  
doi:10.1111/cogs.12878

Borg JM et al.  
*Evolved Open-Endedness in Cultural Evolution: A New Dimension in Open-Ended
Evolution Research.*  
Artificial Life. 2024;30(3):417–441.

## Why it matters

This literature already contains:

- accumulation of modifications;
- differentiation and combination of cultural elements;
- later innovations building on previous elements;
- cultural loss and disappearance;
- recombination;
- cognitive generation of genuinely new representations;
- the distinction between bounded and unbounded cultural evolution.

The cognitive RAF work can trace lineages in which newly generated mental
representations support later hierarchically structured concepts.

The active/history distinction in v17 therefore has strong conceptual ancestry
in cultural retention/loss literature.

## Residual difference

The inspected literature does not supply the full v17 cross-domain theorem
factorization or its finite-envelope/successor-count machinery.

**Preflight classification:** strong antecedent for cumulative reuse and
turnover; not a full duplicate found.

# Strongest antecedent 5 — recombinant technological search

## Representative references

Fleming L, Sorenson O.  
*Technology as a complex adaptive system: evidence from patent data.*  
Research Policy. 2001;30(7):1019–1039.

Fleming L.  
*Recombinant uncertainty in technological search.*  
Management Science. 2001.

Mastrogiorgio / related innovation-search literature; NK-search literature.

Recent synthesis:
*Technology: Theory-driven experimentation and combinatorial salience.*  
European Economic Review. 2026;181:105186.

## Why it matters

Technological innovation has long been described as:

```text
existing building blocks
    -> bounded / difficult recombinant search
    -> new technology
    -> new technology becomes a building block for later technologies.
```

The 2026 work on combinatorial salience makes the finite-search problem
particularly explicit: the combinatorial possibility set can be enormous or
unprestatable, while theory and experimentation make only some combinations
salient enough to search.

This is close to the v17 distinction:

```text
generable possibilities
!= finite admitted/search candidates.
```

## Residual difference

The innovation literature generally treats search, recombination and success
with domain-specific models rather than the full v17 logical interface stack.

**Preflight classification:** strong antecedent for building blocks and bounded
search; not a full duplicate found.

# Strongest antecedent 6 — constructor / formal component-reuse theories

## References

Marletto C.  
*Constructor Theory of Life.*  
arXiv:1407.0681 (2014).

Hill G.  
*Category Theory for the Configuration of Complex Systems.*  
1993.

Hennicker R, Wirsing M.  
*A Formal Method for the Systematic Reuse of Specification Components.*  
1991.

Related algebraic/category-theoretic software/component-reuse literature.

## Why it matters

These frameworks already formalize:

- construction from components;
- recursive composition;
- reusable modules;
- semantic or functional correctness of constructed systems;
- self-reproduction in constructor theory.

They are important antecedents to the new
`ConstructiveRecursiveEmergenceStepAt` distinction.

Therefore EbE should not claim that explicitly linking input components to the
constructed system is itself novel.

## Residual difference

The inspected construction/reuse formalisms generally do not also contain an
evolutionary open-ended chain with independent resource/selection/retention,
essential-parent future-access counterfactuals, finite admission, cumulative
history, and finite-space saturation.

**Preflight classification:** strong construction antecedent; not a full
duplicate found.

# Result of the preflight

## No equal-or-stronger full duplicate was found

This preflight found multiple frameworks that are stronger than EbE on
individual mechanisms and several that come close to the central recursive
intuition.

It did **not** find a single established formal framework that simultaneously
provides the full v17 conjunction.

The strongest challenge is the combination of:

```text
generative RAF / SOR
+
Soros–Stanley OEE opportunity creation
+
co-evolving technological system/search-space models
+
recombinant-search theory.
```

Collectively, that literature contains most of the conceptual ingredients.

That substantially narrows any novelty claim.

## The defensible residue

The strongest defensible research claim after this preflight is:

> **v17 packages a particular cross-domain separation of already-established
> mechanisms into one machine-auditable event architecture, with explicit
> theorem boundaries and countermodels preventing several common
> identifications.**

This is a **formal synthesis / integration claim**, not a claim to have
discovered recursive enabling, cumulative culture, evolving search spaces,
autocatalytic hierarchy, reusable building blocks, or open-ended evolution.

## What this preflight cannot establish

It cannot establish priority.

Literature search is open-ended, terminology differs across disciplines, and
there may be a closer formalism under a different vocabulary.

Therefore the correct review statement is:

```text
No equal-or-stronger full duplicate found in this targeted preflight.
Not evidence that none exists.
```

# Recommendation

**Prior-art gate: pass for broad peer review, with an explicit caveat.**

The internal novelty language is now narrow enough that a broad review would
not be wasting reviewers' time on an obviously rediscovered single mechanism.

The broad review should still include dedicated domain specialists whose job is
to find a stronger antecedent.

The most valuable specialist targets are:

1. RAF / autocatalytic-set theory;
2. artificial-life open-ended evolution;
3. cumulative culture;
4. technological innovation/search;
5. formal construction / category-theoretic component systems.

A discovered stronger antecedent should narrow or replace the EbE framing
rather than be treated defensively.
