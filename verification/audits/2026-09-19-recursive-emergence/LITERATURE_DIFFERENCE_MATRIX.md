# v17 literature-difference matrix

**Purpose:** give reviewers a compact map of what Evolution by Emergence (EbE)
borrows from established science and what role remains for the v17 formal core.

This table is deliberately conservative. “EbE role” does not mean “first
discovery.”

| Topic | Established antecedent / neighboring field | Already established outside EbE | EbE v17 role | EbE does **not** claim |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Variation, selection, retention | evolutionary theory; population genetics | variants differ; filtering/selection changes frequencies; inheritance supports cumulative change | treats generation, validation/selection, and retention as separate generic interfaces | invention of Darwinian selection |
| Open-ended evolution | Banzhaf et al.; Taylor; Packard et al.; Adams et al.; Hernández-Orozco et al. | multiple OEE notions; novelty classes; finite-system limits; state-dependent mechanisms; exploratory/expansive/transformational distinctions | formalizes one narrower endpoint: cumulative retained novelty, plus a sufficient recursive route | a unique or universal definition of OEE |
| Adjacent possible | Kauffman tradition; Tria/Loreto/Servedio/Strogatz | novelty can trigger additional possibilities and dynamically restructure the sampled possibility space | separates new generability from finite admission, realization, filtering, and retention | discovery that novelty can open possibilities |
| Changing phase spaces | Longo, Montévil, Kauffman | biological evolution may alter pertinent observables/phase spaces; stronger claims of unprestatability exist | uses finite moving local envelopes in one ambient capacity type; keeps representational vocabulary change separate | formal proof of unprestatable ontology creation |
| Evolutionary novelty vs innovation | Erwin; evo-devo literature | novelty can be distinct from later ecological/evolutionary success | keeps compositional emergence separate from validation, retention, usefulness and success | discovery of the novelty/innovation distinction |
| Co-option / exaptation | Gould/Vrba tradition; True & Carroll; later co-option literature | existing traits/modules are reused in new contexts | formal parent reuse and optional essential-parent counterfactual | discovery that old components can acquire new uses |
| Evolvability / modularity | Wagner, Altenberg, modularity/facilitated-variation work | organization of variation can affect future evolvability; reusable modules matter | expresses retained primitives as explicit inputs to later generation/accessibility | invention of evolvability or modular reuse |
| Autocatalytic / RAF organization | Kauffman; Hordijk; Steel; Peng et al.; generative RAFs | self-sustaining sets; hierarchical/generative RAF construction; lower tiers enable higher tiers; reaction ordering constraints | offers a cross-domain filter/retention/reuse/search decomposition that RAF systems may instantiate | discovery of recursive enabling or autocatalytic hierarchy |
| Major transitions | Maynard Smith & Szathmáry; transitions literature | new higher-level units and information systems can arise and become evolutionary actors | permits but does not require a new Darwinian individual; asks whether retained organization changes later generability | replacement for major-transitions theory |
| Cumulative culture | Tomasello/Tennie; Enquist et al.; Mesoudi; cultural-evolution models | retained/transmitted innovations permit later modification; cultural loss and acquisition costs matter | separates active repertoire from cumulative historical trace; provides generic generation/filter/retention bookkeeping | discovery of the ratchet effect |
| Technological recombination | Fleming; Sorenson; Arthur; innovation studies | technologies recombine; new technologies become building blocks; inherited repertoire shapes future search | explicit construction, essential-parent test, finite admission, and retained-success interfaces | discovery of recombinant technological evolution |
| Co-evolving repertoire and search space | Winters & Charbonneau | open-ended cultural/technological growth can depend on co-evolution of technological/cultural systems and search spaces under resource/selection conditions | theorem-oriented local event factorization rather than macro-dynamical simulation | discovery that search spaces themselves can evolve |
| Viability / reachability | Aubin and control/viability theory | reachable/viable sets under constraints are mature mathematics | uses accessibility/envelope concepts as application-specific instances, not replacements | invention of constrained reachability |
| Resource-limited maintenance | ecology, chemostats, synthetic-biology burden, reliability/control | maintenance and shared resources constrain persistence and response | connects existing resource/slack results to a separate recursive-innovation interface without identifying them | proof that more resources automatically cause innovation |
| Reproduction thresholds | branching processes; next-generation methods | reproduction numbers and criticality thresholds are mature | local deterministic R_E is explicitly the cardinality of actual full successors; ledger is separated from count | a new stochastic branching theorem |
| Component construction / reuse | engineering configuration; software/component reuse; systems composition | complex systems can be recursively constructed from reusable components with formal composition semantics | adds optional configuration-level bridge so generated parent construction and emergent realization can share the same configuration witness | invention of modular construction |
| Formal verification | theorem-proving/formal-methods traditions | proof assistants can audit deductive claims and interfaces | provides a Lean-checked theorem/countermodel surface for this particular synthesis | empirical validation or proof of scientific priority |

## The difference in one paragraph

The v17 candidate does not ask reviewers to accept a new physical force or a
new replacement for evolutionary theory.

Its proposed role is narrower:

> **take mechanisms that are established across several fields, express their
> interfaces so they cannot be silently conflated, machine-check selected
> sufficient implications and counterexamples, and provide one common
> architecture for asking how realized organization becomes retained reusable
> material that changes later accessibility.**

The scientific value of that architecture depends on whether it:

1. makes cross-field comparisons easier;
2. exposes assumptions that prose normally hides;
3. yields useful domain mappings;
4. generates testable questions;
5. remains precise under adversarial review.

If an existing framework already provides this full role more clearly and more
strongly, EbE should cite and defer to it rather than defend a priority claim.
